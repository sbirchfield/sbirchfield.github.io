Quiz 3: Thresholding, Erosion, and Dilation

Score: 0 / 4

. What does thresholding do to a grayscale image?

Thresholding is a per-pixel comparison against a cutoff value, producing a binary image.

. What criterion does Otsu's method use to automatically choose a threshold?

Otsu searches every possible cutoff for the one that best separates the histogram into two low-variance classes.

. What effect does erosion have on a white region in a binary image?

Erosion only keeps a pixel white if the entire structuring element fits inside the white region, so regions shrink.

. What is "opening" (erosion followed by dilation) typically used for?

Erosion first wipes out small specks entirely; the following dilation grows the surviving (larger) regions back toward their original size.
