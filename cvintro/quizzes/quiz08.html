Quiz 8: Geometric Transformations

Score: 0 / 4

. What is the key difference between a Euclidean transform and a similarity transform?

Euclidean transforms (rotation + translation) preserve exact distances; similarity transforms add a single uniform scale factor, so they preserve angles and length ratios but not absolute distances.

. Which of the three transform types (Euclidean, similarity, affine) is capable of shearing a square into a parallelogram?

Affine transforms allow any invertible 2x2 matrix, including shear, which neither Euclidean nor similarity transforms permit.

. What does a similarity transform preserve that a general affine transform does not necessarily preserve?

Similarity transforms preserve angles and length ratios; affine transforms give up angle preservation in exchange for allowing shear.

. How many point correspondences does cv2.getAffineTransform require to solve for all 6 affine parameters?

3 point correspondences give exactly 6 equations, matching the 6 free parameters of an affine transform.
